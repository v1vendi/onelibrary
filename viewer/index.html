OneLibrary Viewer

decrypts rekordbox exports in your browser

Drop a rekordbox device here

Drag a USB stick or SD card onto this page to browse its tracks, playlists, cues and waveforms.

Dropping just exportLibrary.db works too, but without the analysis files there are no waveforms.

Opening the device lets edits be saved back to it in place. Chrome and Edge only.

Nothing is uploaded. The database is decrypted here in the page using WebCrypto, and never leaves this machine.
